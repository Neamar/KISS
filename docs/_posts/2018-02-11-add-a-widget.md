KISS only supports widgets in `Minimalistic UI`.

## Enable `Minimalistic UI`

From the settings, enable `Minimalistic UI` (`⋮, KISS settings, Home screen`, enable `Minimalistic UI`).

While you're there, you may want to enable some other settings in the `Minimalistic UI` section to make sure you're happy with the end result!

Go back to KISS by pressing the Home button on your device.

## Add a widget
Press the three-dots menu again `⋮`. You should now see a new option in the menu, "Add widget":

![Menu: Add widget](/screenshots/widget-context-menu.png)

From there, pick a widget in the list. Android may ask you to allow KISS to create widgets, and some widgets will open their own configuration screen first.

![Widget picker](/screenshots/widget-picker.png)

The widget will then appear at the top of the screen, above the empty search bar. You can add as many widgets as you want: they're stacked vertically, each new widget being added below the previous ones.

![Home screen with two widgets](/screenshots/example-widget.png)

## Customize your widgets
Long-press on a widget to display its menu:

![Widget menu](/screenshots/widget-long-press-menu.png)

* `Settings` reopens the widget's configuration screen (only displayed for widgets that can be reconfigured);
* `Enlarge` and `Reduce` change the height of the widget, within the limits allowed by the widget;
* `Move up` and `Move down` change the order of your widgets;
* `Remove widget` removes the widget.

If you want some room between your widgets, use `Space between widgets` in `⋮, KISS settings, Home screen`.

## Scroll through your widgets
When your widgets don't fit on the screen, scroll through them with a two-finger swipe. Touching a widget with one finger still interacts with the widget itself.
